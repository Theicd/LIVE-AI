A — Manifesto / 2025 Reading time · 38 sec

We do not believe in neutral design. Every surface, every line of copy, every millisecond of load time is a decision about what kind of future a brand is choosing to build. Our work is slow on purpose — we read the brief twice, we walk the factory floor, we redraw the wordmark forty times before we show it. The result is not louder. It is more precise, and therefore harder to imitate, and therefore — finally — worth remembering.

— The Studio, Brooklyn Document 001 / Rev. 14

C — Principles of Practice

Six commitments
we will not
negotiate.

These are not slogans. They are the operating constraints that have kept the studio intact through fourteen years, three recessions, and one pandemic.

01 — Slowness

We work in weeks, not sprints.

The most valuable brand decisions are the ones a founder has to sit with for seven days before approving. We structure our process to protect that silence.

02 — Material

Every output is a real object.

A logo is printed on a box. A website is rendered on a 2017 phone in a subway tunnel. We test every system against the worst-case physical context before we ship it.

03 — Authorship

One team. One room. One name on the work.

We do not subcontract. We do not white-label. The four people in the kickoff meeting are the four people who hand you the final files.

04 — Restraint

Subtract until it sings.

If a layout still works in greyscale, with all the imagery removed, on a 320px viewport — it is ready to receive colour and photography.

05 — Money

Fixed fee. No surprises.

We quote a single number for the entire engagement, broken into four milestones. If we underestimate the work, that is our problem, not yours.

06 — Refusal

We turn down most projects.

Last year we accepted eleven of the ninety-four enquiries we received. The ones we decline get a personal note explaining why, and a recommendation of who would do it better.
